I work across identity, print, 3D and real-time — in the space where those disciplines stop behaving like separate trades. A logotype has to survive being embroidered and rendered at 12px. A programme has to fold down into a poster. An AR layer has to load before anyone loses interest. The medium keeps changing; the problem of staying legible under pressure does not.

Based in Tehran, working with technology platforms at home and cultural projects abroad. Most things start on paper or on a grid before anything gets rendered, and most arrive as a question about form and leave as a system somebody else can run without me.
